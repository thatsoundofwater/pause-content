p@use Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

p@use is one button that shows a small, calm surprise. It's made by @water.

What we collect

Nothing. p@use has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party ads. We don't collect, store or share any personal information. (Now and then the app mentions our own other apps, ch@nt and bl@nk. Nothing about you is involved.)

What stays on your phone

Your press count, the surprises you save, and your settings (appearance, daily reminder time) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

The button notices things, on your phone only

To feel a little alive, the button reacts to things like tilting or shaking the phone, the battery level, headphones, the volume, and when you take a screenshot. This happens entirely on your phone, needs no permission, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Purchases

p@use+ is a subscription handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel it anytime in Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

New surprises

p@use may download a public list of new surprises from GitHub. This download does not send any information about you.

Notifications

If you turn on "Daily pause", p@use schedules one reminder a day on your phone. You can turn it off anytime in the app or in Settings.

Children

p@use doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
