bl@nk Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

bl@nk is 50+ little AI-powered tools in one app: for when you draw a blank on a song, a movie or a quote, and a lot more. It's made by @water.

The short version

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no selling your data. When a tool needs AI, what you give that tool (a photo, a short sound, or some text) is sent to make the answer. Nothing else.

What gets sent, and only when you use a tool

  • Photos you take or choose for a tool (for example plant and bug ID, outfit feedback, "what is this").
  • Short audio when you ask a tool to listen (for example to name a song).
  • Text you type into a tool.

These are sent securely to our server and to the service that makes the answer, and the answer comes back to your phone. Depending on the tool, that service is OpenAI (photos, text and audio for AI answers), AudD (short audio to identify a song or a hum), ElevenLabs (text turned into a voice), LALAL.AI (audio clips split into stems) or MusicAPI (music generation). We use what you send only to give you that answer. We don't sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to identify you. Each provider handles it under its own privacy policy.

Location

Some tools use your location when you tap "use my location" or allow access. Direction Finder and Golden Hour use it on your phone only (Direction Finder with Apple Maps). Date Idea, Best Day This Week and Night Sky send your coordinates, or the city you type, through our server to a weather or places service so it can give you local results. We use it only for that request, we don't store it, and we don't sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to identify you.

Music, movies and other public info

Some tools fetch public information (like song details or movie info). These requests don't include anything about you.

Music previews, stems and copyright

Song ID uses the short preview clips provided by public music catalogs. bl@nk does not provide full songs. A tool can separate a 30-second preview into vocal and instrumental tracks, and you can save or share those files. They are for your personal, creative use, such as learning, practice and sampling ideas. Do not post, sell, stream or release other people's music, or audio made from it, without permission from the rights holder. You are responsible for how you use and share what you create. Rights holders can contact us below.

What stays on your phone

Your saved items (favorites, saved QR codes, covers, journals, history) and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Any purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

bl@nk is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
