@ura Support

Point the camera at anyone or anything, tap check aura, and see their score.

Are the scores real?

No. Every score is random and just for fun. That's the point.

How do duels work?

Get two faces in the frame and it becomes a duel. Three or more people get ranked. Put your pet next to you and see who wins.

Can I score an old photo?

Yes. Tap the photos button in @ura, or open a photo in the Photos app, tap Share and choose @ura.

The camera isn't showing

Go to Settings › @ura and turn on Camera.
