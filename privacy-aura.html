@ura Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

@ura is a camera that gives anything you point it at a random aura score, just for fun. It's made by @water.

The short version

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your photos never leave your phone unless you choose to share them.

Camera and photos

@ura uses the camera to take the picture it scores. If you pick a photo from your library, or share one to @ura from the Photos app, only that photo is used. Everything, including spotting faces, pets and scenes, happens on your phone with Apple's built-in tools. Nothing is uploaded, and the scores are random, not a real analysis of you.

Reaction shots

While a reading plays, @ura may use the front camera for a few seconds to catch your reaction for the reveal video. Those frames stay on your phone and are only used in the video you make.

What stays on your phone

Your hall of fame, hall of shame, secret auras found and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

When you share a reveal, it goes through Apple's share sheet to the app you choose. We never see it. Saving to Photos asks for your permission first.

New lines

Now and then the app downloads a public file of new aura lines from our content page. Nothing about you is sent with it.

Children

@ura is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
