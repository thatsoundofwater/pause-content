ch@nt Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ch@nt says your own affirmations back to you, in a calm voice you choose. It's made by @water.

The short version

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. To turn your words into a voice, the text you write is sent to our voice service. That's it.

Your words and the voice

When you create an affirmation, its text and the voice you picked are sent securely to our server, which asks ElevenLabs (our voice provider) to speak it. The audio comes back to your phone. We use your text only to make that audio. We don't sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to identify you. ElevenLabs handles the text under its own privacy policy (elevenlabs.io/privacy).

Speaking instead of typing

If you use the microphone to dictate, Apple's speech recognition turns your voice into text. Apple handles that audio under Apple's privacy policy. ch@nt never records or keeps your voice.

What stays on your phone

Your affirmations, sessions, streaks, sound mix and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Any purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

ch@nt is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
